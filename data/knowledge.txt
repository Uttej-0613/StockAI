The stock market allows investors to buy and sell shares of companies.

A stock represents ownership in a company.

Volume means the number of shares traded during a period.

Market capitalization is the total value of a company's shares.

P/E ratio compares a company's share price to its earnings.

Long term investing usually involves holding stocks for years.

Diversification reduces risk by investing in different sectors.

Blue chip stocks are financially strong and stable companies.

Technical analysis studies price charts and trends.

Fundamental analysis studies financial health of companies.
